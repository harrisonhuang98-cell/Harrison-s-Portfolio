About Harrison

Computer science.
Human impact.

A student exploring where computing meets biomedical engineering.

I’m Harrison Huang, a computer science student with an interest in biomedical engineering. I’m curious about how software, artificial intelligence, and engineering can help people understand and improve human health.

I’m at the beginning of my learning journey. My focus is on developing a foundation in programming, logical thinking, and problem solving, while exploring how those skills could apply to medical technology. I’m especially interested in how AI may shape the future of biomedical engineering.

I want to learn how to break a complex problem into smaller steps, evaluate possible solutions, and communicate my reasoning clearly. As I gain experience, I plan to document what I try, what I learn, and how I improve my approach.

I haven’t completed any portfolio projects yet. This website is a starting point for sharing future projects, research, and skills, with space for examples that show my progress. I’m interested in learning from experienced teams and exploring student opportunities in computer science, biomedical engineering, and healthcare technology.
